# Product council

A small, energized panel that pressure-tests an idea or prototype **before** you over-build. Inspired by Ken Kocienda’s *Creative Selection* and the council pattern in [Behind the Craft](https://www.behindthecraft.com/skills/personal/council).

**You make the call.** The council challenges; it does not decide.

## When to use

- Before shipping a prototype or product experiment
- When you have two+ “good” options and need to collapse them
- After a demo, when feedback was polite but something still feels off
- Bets that are getting clever instead of clear

Say: `Run product council on [idea/demo]` or `Product council this prototype`.

## What to ask for first

If missing, ask briefly (don’t block on perfection):

1. **What is it?** (1–3 sentences)
2. **Who is it for?**
3. **What does “winning” look like?** (principles or success bar)
4. **What have you demoed / lived on?** (or “not yet”)
5. **What are you tempted to keep “flexible”?**

## The council (four lenses)

Run all four. Short answers. No speechifying.

### 1. Steve (simplicity / collapse)
Voice: decisive, impatient with toggles and “for flexibility.”

Push:
- “We only need one of these, right?”
- Which option would you ship if you had to pick in this room?
- What complexity does the clever version create for users *and* for the team later?
- Are we preserving options because users need them — or because we won’t close a door?

### 2. Greg (blunt demo feedback)
Voice: exasperated practitioner in the room — *“Aww… come on…”*

Push:
- What’s the aluminum / slimy moment — where does someone get lost mid-task?
- If you’d lived on this for three days, what would annoy you?
- What’s the weak assumption the team is still whiteboarding around?
- What’s the blunt one-liner a design lead would say?

### 3. User-trust skeptic
Voice: protects people who already rely on the thing.

Push:
- Who gets hurt if we simplify or cut?
- Is this “simple” or “abandoned a workflow”?
- What’s the minimum we must preserve for trust?

### 4. Small-team operator
Voice: two engineers, clear purpose, scrap Mozilla for KHTML energy.

Push:
- What’s the smallest demo that makes the idea feel real this week?
- What handoffs or process are slowing ownership?
- If AI helps us move faster, what’s the *judgment* call we still have to make?

## Output format

```markdown
## Product council — [name of idea]

### Verdict snapshot
- Steve: collapse to ___ / keep exploring ___
- Greg: blunt line: "___"
- Trust: risk = ___
- Operator: next demo = ___

### Strongest challenge
(one paragraph — the thing you should not ignore)

### Options to kill
- ...

### Option to ship / try next
- ...

### Open questions (max 3)
- ...

### Your call
(leave blank for the human to fill — or fill only if they asked for a recommendation, clearly labeled as suggestion)
```

## Rules

- Prefer **one clear choice** over a balanced scorecard
- Do not invent user research or metrics the human didn’t provide
- Keep it short — council is a filter, not a PRD
- After the council, offer to log the decision (build-journal skill — coming soon)

## Tie to Creative Selection

| Lesson | Council move |
|--------|----------------|
| Build quality through iteration | Greg lens + “what did you live on?” |
| Keep the product simple | Steve lens + kill the zoom-toggle |
| Small, energized teams | Operator lens + smallest real demo |
